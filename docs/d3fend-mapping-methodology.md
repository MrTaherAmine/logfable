# D3FEND Mapping Methodology

Baseline: MITRE D3FEND ontology 1.5.0, published 31 July 2026. Bundled D3FEND IDs are checked against the project’s pinned reviewed index.

Each mapping records ATT&CK ID/version, D3FEND ID/version, relationship, mapping type, evidence, rationale, confidence, source URL, review state, and reviewer. Project-curated relationships are clearly separated from official material. D3FEND’s ATT&CK relationships may be inferred/experimental; LogFable does not present them as guaranteed mitigations.
